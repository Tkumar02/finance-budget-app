Finnexa-budget is a finance tool
It's function is to give helpful estimates for financial planning

Features of the app:
1. Ability to input:
    a. Age
    b. All incomes, whether PAYE or self employed and all expenses related to self employed income
    c. All outgoints, whether monthly or annual
    d. All savings, capital and monthly input
    e. Monthly mortgage overpayments
    f. estimated costs at retirement
    g. age at retirement
    h. estimate rate of growth of investments
    i. estimated % of drawdown of the pots
    j. input of pension contribution and whether this is NHS / Civil service / Teachers' pension and add this automatically to the retirement pension pots
2. The app will then be able to tell you:
    a. what is your suprlus each month
    b. what will your pot look like at retirement age
    c. which pots you can legally drawdown from and what the penalties might be
    d. if you have a self employed income what you need to save monthly to pay tax
    e. if you're above the 100k threshold what you should contribute to SIPP to bring this down 
    f. what your retirement income would be based on your pot size and % you want to draw down from it
